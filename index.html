TaskStick gives you a fast, beautiful interface for your Google Tasks. You can:

  • Create new lists and add tasks to them in seconds
  • Star tasks to pin them to your Priority view
  • Add due dates and notes to any task
  • Set up a Daily Ritual checklist that resets itself every day
  • Everything syncs instantly with Google Tasks — nothing lives only in this app

Apple Reminders (Mac)
⬇️
1. Download & open
Download the helper app, unzip it, drag it to Applications, then open it. macOS will warn it's from an unidentified developer — right-click the app → Open → Open again to confirm (only needed the first time).
🔔
2. Allow Reminders access
macOS will ask for permission to access Reminders — click Allow and let the dialog fully resolve. Look for a small TaskStick icon in the menu bar once it's running.
🔗
3. Connect it
Settings → Apple Reminders → Enable Apple Reminders Sync, then Open in TaskStick Reminders Helper. Pick which lists to sync — matching Reminders lists are created automatically.
🛠️
Troubleshooting
Click the menu bar icon → Check Reminders Permission… for a clear status. Sync runs automatically every 10 minutes, or click Sync Now anytime.
