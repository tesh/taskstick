Plan my day from TaskStick:

1. Get my Priority tasks with `tasks_list_priority`.
2. Find tasks due today or overdue across my lists (use `tasks_list_tasklists`, then `tasks_list_tasks` on the lists that have them; skip "Daily Ritual").
3. Give me a short plan: at most five things to do today in a sensible order, each with one line on why. Flag anything overdue.
4. Ask whether to star, snooze or reschedule anything before changing tasks.
