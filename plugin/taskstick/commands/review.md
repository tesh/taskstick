description: Weekly review of your TaskStick lists
---

Run a weekly review of my TaskStick tasks:

1. Open the board with `tasks_open_board` so I can see everything.
2. Point out: overdue tasks, starred tasks that have sat for a long time, snoozed tasks worth bringing back, and likely duplicates.
3. Suggest changes as one numbered list (complete, snooze, unsnooze, star, re-date, merge). Don't change anything until I say which numbers to apply, then apply exactly those.
